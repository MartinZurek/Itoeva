# Wildtiere für Fennecs Ruf – Bildprovenienz

Die 16 Zeichnungen wurden mit dem eingebauten ImageGen erstellt. Stilreferenz war der vorhandene Fennec-Kampfatlas. Die Bilder wurden als transparentes PNG generiert und ausschließlich über vermessene Quellrechtecke in das Spiel integriert; kein Nachzeichnen oder Hochskalieren kleiner Vorschauzellen.

## Erster Generierungstext

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production transparent 4-by-4 animation sprite atlas for the painted 2.5D fantasy game Itoeva. Supporting style reference: attached Fennec combat atlas, only its warm gouache watercolor brush texture, finely penciled organic contours, realistic readable animal anatomy and rich storybook color; DO NOT redraw Fennec. Create exactly sixteen separate full-body animal sprites on a strict equal 4-column by 4-row grid, square 2048x2048 image, each cell 512x512, all on genuine transparent background, no letters, labels, borders or scenery. Row 1: small forest tabby cat. Row 2: sturdy wild boar. Row 3: large majestic grey timber wolf. Row 4: powerful dark silverback gorilla. Each row shows SAME animal individual in FOUR distinct sequential attack poses, always facing right: column 1 running approach with compressed stance, column 2 forward leap / windup, column 3 fully extended attack (cat paw swipe, boar head-first charge, wolf forward pounce with forepaws, gorilla long powerful arm strike), column 4 recovery landing / deceleration. Keep each entire animal within its own cell with at least 55 transparent pixels on all four cell edges. Feet align at 440px within each cell. Same design, size and palette across each row; cat smaller with furred agile body, boar stocky and low, wolf longer elegant body not fox proportions, gorilla grounded heavy muscular anatomy with believable hands. Detailed fur, expressive determined faces, anatomically coherent limbs, separate clear poses, subtle motion in fur. No costumes, no weapons, no blood, no magic effects, no shadows outside sprites, no cartoon emoji, no chunky pixel art, no background. Fine handmade warm fantasy art matching the reference, not a pasted flat sticker. Output real alpha transparency, exact regular grid and sixteen animals only.

## Korrekturtext für Abstände

Edit target: the transparent wildlife attack atlas. Preserve the sixteen designs and exact same handmade fur/art quality, same tabby cat, boar, grey wolf and silverback gorilla, same four attack phases per row, same right-facing orientation. Fix ONLY spacing and alignment so it is a usable game sprite atlas: exact four equal columns, four equal rows, each animal centered inside its own cell at roughly 65% of the cell width, no pixel of any animal near or across the cell border, at least 12% cell width clear transparent gutter on every cell edge. Especially shrink and recenter extended wolf, boar, cat and gorilla strike poses; align every animal's feet at 80% cell height. Full bodies must fit, nothing clipped. Keep transparent background, no text, no gridlines, no scenery. Leave large transparent gutters between all sixteen sprites. Do not change species or designs. Real alpha transparency.

## Tatsächliches Ergebnis

Das Modell lieferte 1254×1254 Pixel und keine vollständig gleichmäßigen Rasterzellen. Deshalb benutzt die Runtime die in wildlife_atlas.json festgehaltenen 16 tatsächlichen Alphaausschnitte. Der Originalatlas bleibt unverändert. Gemeinsame Referenzbreiten pro Tier und feste Fußpunkte halten den Maßstab stabil.
