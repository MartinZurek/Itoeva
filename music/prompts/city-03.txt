Melancholic instrumental French hip hop beat for a pixel city at dusk -
rainy streets, quiet thoughts, a little nostalgia and a lot of heart. 88
BPM in 4/4 with heavy, dusty boom bap drums on every beat from the first
bar to the last.

A sad upright piano plays one simple four-bar loop in C minor, and the
same loop repeats for the whole piece over the chords C minor, A flat
major, E flat major, G major. Soft strings hold long notes underneath and
a warm musette accordion answers each loop with a short, simple phrase. A
deep, round bass follows the roots; the drums hit hard but stay laid back:
thick kick, snapping snare on two and four, swung hi-hats.

On every second pass a soft 8-bit square-wave voice doubles the piano
melody one octave higher, like a street lamp flickering in pixels. Every
instrument stays perfectly in tune and in time, with no pitch wobble. The
mix is warm and close, clear in the mid range, never harsh.

The piece starts at full level and keeps it: no intro, no ending, no
fade-out. The last bar leads straight back into the first so the 90-second
loop is seamless.

No vocals, no rap, no spoken word. No drops, no risers, no dramatic
climax.
