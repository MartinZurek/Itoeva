Melancholic instrumental French hip hop beat for a pixel city at dusk -
rainy streets, quiet thoughts, a little nostalgia and a lot of heart. 88
BPM in 4/4 with heavy, dusty boom bap drums on every beat from the first
bar to the last.

A sad upright piano plays one simple four-bar loop in C minor, and the
same loop repeats for the whole piece over the chords C minor, A flat
major, E flat major, G major. Soft strings hold long notes underneath and
a warm musette accordion answers each loop with a short, simple phrase. A
deep, round bass follows the roots; the drums hit hard but stay laid back:
thick kick, snapping snare on two and four, swung hi-hats.

In the second half, a soft, rounded triangle-wave 8-bit voice answers the
melody with its own short phrase in the same middle register, like a
street lamp flickering in pixels, taking turns with the lead rather than
playing along with it. Halfway through, a contrasting four-bar B phrase at
the same energy gives the main melody a rest - a different lead instrument
and a new chord colour from the same key - and then the main melody
returns. Every instrument stays perfectly in tune and in time, with no
pitch wobble. The mix is warm and close, clear in the mid range, never
harsh.

Harmony and tone: one key from the first bar to the last, simple diatonic
chords in consonant voicings, every voice perfectly in tune with every
other. The lead and the keys stay in the warm middle register, and the few
high notes are soft and rounded. A clean, open tone with generous headroom
and a gentle, soft top end.

The piece starts at full level and keeps it: no intro, no ending, no
fade-out. The last bar leads straight back into the first so the 90-second
loop is seamless.

No vocals, no rap, no spoken word. No drops, no risers, no dramatic
climax.
