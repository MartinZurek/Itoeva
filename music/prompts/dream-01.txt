Vast, tender instrumental piece for a pixel world under a sky full of
stars - awe, longing and quiet hope, like looking up at night and missing
someone far away. 72 BPM in 4/4 with a soft, steady pulse on every beat
from the first bar to the last.

A warm pipe organ plays one simple, repeating pattern of broken chords in
E minor, and the same four-bar pattern returns for the whole piece. A
clear piano plays a slow, singable melody of long notes above it. Soft
sustained strings hold the chords: E minor, C major, G major, D major. A
quiet wooden tick, like a small clock, marks every beat.

On every second pass a soft 8-bit square-wave voice doubles the piano
melody one octave higher, like a distant star blinking in pixels. The
sound is wide, warm and calm, clear in the mid range, never harsh, with no
deep rumble.

The piece starts at full level and keeps it: no intro, no ending, no
fade-out. The last bar leads straight back into the first so the 90-second
loop is seamless.

No vocals, no choir, no spoken word. No drums, no drops, no risers, no
dramatic climax.
