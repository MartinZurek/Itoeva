Epic, motivating instrumental piece where a full orchestra meets hip hop
drums, for a pixel hero in training - courage, strength and quiet pride.
90 BPM in 4/4 with huge, steady drums on every beat from the first bar to
the last.

Low strings drive a relentless sixteenth-note ostinato in G minor, and the
same two-bar pattern continues for the whole piece. Bold French horns play
one heroic, singable four-bar theme on top over the chords G minor, E flat
major, B flat major, F major, and the same theme returns every four bars.
The drums are big hip hop drums with orchestral weight: deep kick, a
massive snare on two and four, and low orchestral percussion hits on the
first beat of every bar.

In the second half, a soft, rounded triangle-wave 8-bit voice answers the
horn theme with its own short phrase in the same middle register, like a
hero drawn in pixels, taking turns with the lead rather than playing along
with it. Halfway through, a contrasting four-bar B phrase at the same
energy gives the main melody a rest - a different lead instrument and a
new chord colour from the same key - and then the main melody returns. The
sound is powerful and wide but clear, never harsh, and stays at the same
strong energy from start to end.

Harmony and tone: one key from the first bar to the last, simple diatonic
chords in consonant voicings, every voice perfectly in tune with every
other. The lead and the keys stay in the warm middle register, and the few
high notes are soft and rounded. A clean, open tone with generous headroom
and a gentle, soft top end.

The piece starts at full level and keeps it: no intro, no ending, no
fade-out. The last bar leads straight back into the first so the 90-second
loop is seamless.

No vocals, no choir, no spoken word. No drops, no risers, no build-up.
