Bright, catchy instrumental chiptune for a pixel arcade - the thrill of the
first coin, blinking lights and a new high score within reach. 132 BPM in
4/4 with a punchy, clearly audible pulse on every beat from the first bar
to the last.

A square-wave lead plays one short, instantly memorable four-bar melody in
C major, and the same melody returns for the whole piece. A second pulse
voice answers each phrase with a quick arpeggio. A triangle-wave bass
bounces on the root and fifth of the chords C major, A minor, F major, G
major, and noise-channel drums keep a tight beat: kick on one and three,
snare on two and four, fast hi-hat ticks.

Every sound is clean 8-bit chip music, perfectly in tune and in time. The
mix is bright and crisp, clear in the mid range, never harsh.

The piece starts at full level and keeps it: no intro, no ending, no
fade-out. The last bar leads straight back into the first so the 90-second
loop is seamless.

No vocals, no spoken word, no sound effects, no game-over jingles. No
drops, no risers.
