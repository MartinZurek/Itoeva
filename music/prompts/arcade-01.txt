Cheerful, catchy instrumental chiptune for a pixel arcade - the thrill of
the first coin, blinking lights and a new high score within reach. 132 BPM
in 4/4 with a punchy, clearly audible pulse on every beat from the first
bar to the last.

A soft, rounded pulse-wave lead plays one short, instantly memorable
four-bar melody in C major, and the same melody returns for the whole
piece. A second pulse voice answers each phrase with a quick arpeggio in
the same register, never above the lead. A triangle-wave bass bounces on
the root and fifth of the chords C major, A minor, F major, G major, and
noise-channel drums keep a tight beat: kick on one and three, snare on two
and four, fast hi-hat ticks.

Every sound is clean 8-bit chip music, perfectly in tune and in time. The
mix is warm and crisp, clear in the mid range, never harsh. Halfway
through, a contrasting four-bar B phrase at the same energy gives the main
melody a rest - a different lead instrument and a new chord colour from
the same key - and then the main melody returns.

Harmony and tone: one key from the first bar to the last, simple diatonic
chords in consonant voicings, every voice perfectly in tune with every
other. The lead and the keys stay in the warm middle register, and the few
high notes are soft and rounded. A clean, open tone with generous headroom
and a gentle, soft top end.

The piece starts at full level and keeps it: no intro, no ending, no
fade-out. The last bar leads straight back into the first so the 90-second
loop is seamless.

No vocals, no spoken word, no sound effects, no game-over jingles. No
drops, no risers.
