Clear, orderly instrumental character theme for a quiet and patient pixel
creature who observes before acting - the most precise and focused of six
personal themes, calm and warm rather than cold.

Tempo around 78 BPM with a steady, restrained pulse. A clean vibraphone states
the central motif with soft mallets and natural resonance; a close felt piano
answers in the lower middle register. Use a small rounded bass, muted rim
clicks and the lightest closed shaker to mark time without crowding it. Every
part should enter deliberately, leave space, and return to its exact place.

The melody is the point of this piece: a simple, singable eight-bar motif with
four clear notes in its opening cell and a patient descending answer. Repeat it
several times with one controlled change per pass - shift one note upward,
move the answer to piano, then restore the original shape. The motif must
remain immediately recognizable throughout. A person should be able to learn
it by ear and sing it later, although this recording is entirely instrumental.

Use open, warm harmony and gentle vibraphone decay so the precision never feels
clinical. The mood is attentive, observant and quietly encouraging, like
noticing a small detail and giving it time. Keep dynamics even and align the
last pulse and resonance with the opening for a seamless 90-second loop.

No vocals, no rap, no spoken word, no choir, no vocal samples. No frantic
syncopation, no busy arpeggios, no virtuosic runs, no dramatic climax, no
cinematic or orchestral scoring, no distorted guitars, no heavy drums, no EDM
drops, builds or risers, no icy glass textures, no sterile metronome effect,
no shapeless ambient drift, no harsh transients, no vinyl crackle. Nothing
hurried, cluttered or emotionally cold.
