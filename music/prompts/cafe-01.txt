Warm, cosy instrumental cafe piece for a small pixel coffee house on a
lazy afternoon - the smell of cocoa, steam from the espresso machine,
quiet chatter and a seat by the window. 96 BPM in 4/4 with a gentle
swing and a clearly audible pulse on every beat from the first bar to the
last.

An accordion plays one simple, charming four-bar melody in B-flat major,
and the same melody returns for the whole piece. A nylon-string guitar
plays light swing chords B-flat major, G minor, E-flat major, F major in
a repeating cycle, a warm upright bass walks under them, a soft vibraphone
answers the melody on every second phrase in the same middle register,
and brushed drums keep an easy, relaxed swing.

In the second half, a soft, rounded triangle-wave 8-bit voice answers
the melody with its own short phrase in the same middle register, like
steam drawn in pixels, taking turns with the lead rather than playing
along with it. Halfway through, a contrasting four-bar B phrase at the
same energy gives the main melody a rest - a different lead instrument
and a new chord colour from the same key - and then the main melody
returns. The sound is warm, intimate and relaxed, clear in the mid range,
never harsh.

Harmony and tone: one key from the first bar to the last, simple diatonic
chords in consonant voicings, every voice perfectly in tune with every
other. The lead and the keys stay in the warm middle register, and the few
high notes are soft and rounded. A clean, open tone with generous headroom
and a gentle, soft top end.

The piece starts at full level and keeps it: no intro, no ending, no
fade-out. The last bar leads straight back into the first so the 90-second
loop is seamless.

No vocals, no spoken word, no cafe noises, no cup or machine sounds. No
drops, no risers.
