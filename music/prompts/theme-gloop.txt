Cozy, gently off-kilter instrumental character theme for a relaxed and
slightly chaotic pixel creature who helps everything slow down - the slowest
and softest-edged of six personal themes.

Tempo around 56 BPM in a loose, swaying four-four pulse. A round, soft electric
bass carries the foundation with long notes and an occasional friendly slide.
Warm, washed synthesizer pads breathe in and out behind it without becoming a
featureless drone. Use muted brush percussion, a very soft kick and small
shaker gestures with no sharp attacks. A mellow bass clarinet or rounded analog
lead carries the tune above the low texture.

The melody is the point of this piece: a simple, singable eight-bar tune made
from long, rounded notes and one playful three-note turn. Keep the first phrase
clear, let the answer arrive a little late, and allow one harmless extra note
to wander in before the final cadence. Repeat the tune with small changes in
colour, not in identity. A person should be able to learn it by ear and sing it
later, although this recording is entirely instrumental.

Harmony should feel cushioned and safe, using warm sevenths, suspended tones
and slow voice leading. The slight disorder is affectionate rather than
random: a phrase leans behind the beat, a pad blooms unevenly, then everything
settles again. Preserve plenty of space for game sounds and balance the final
decay with the opening so the 90-second loop is seamless.

No vocals, no rap, no spoken word, no choir, no vocal samples. No hard-edged
drums, no bright plucks, no distorted guitars, no frantic syncopation, no
dramatic climax, no cinematic or orchestral scoring, no EDM drops, builds or
risers, no gloomy drone, no shapeless ambient wash, no comic slapstick sounds,
no harsh transients, no vinyl crackle. Nothing hurried, rigid or anxious.
