Cheerful, relaxed instrumental shop music for a small pixel supermarket -
friendly, a little cheeky, the tune everyone hums while browsing the
shelves. 110 BPM in 4/4 with a light, bouncy bossa nova groove on every
beat from the first bar to the last.

A warm electric piano plays one catchy, singable four-bar melody in C
major, and the same melody returns for the whole piece. A marimba answers
each phrase with a playful little run. A nylon-string guitar plays the
chords C major 7, A minor 7, D minor 7, G 7 in a steady bossa pattern,
a round upright bass bounces on the root and fifth, and soft brushed
drums with a rim click and a shaker keep time.

On every second pass a soft 8-bit square-wave voice doubles the melody
one octave higher, like a price tag blinking in pixels. The sound is
clean, bright and friendly, clear in the mid range, never harsh.

The piece starts at full level and keeps it: no intro, no ending, no
fade-out. The last bar leads straight back into the first so the 90-second
loop is seamless.

No vocals, no choir, no spoken word, no announcements. No drops, no
risers, no dramatic climax.
