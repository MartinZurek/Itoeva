childlike curiosity, gentle magic and a little nostalgia, like a hand-drawn
animated film about a hidden forest spirit. 92 BPM in a lilting 3/4 waltz
with a soft, clearly audible pulse on every beat from the first bar to the
last.

wooden flute answers each phrase with a short echo, a harp plays gentle
broken chords on every beat, and soft pizzicato cellos mark the first beat

On every second pass a soft 8-bit square-wave voice doubles the piano
melody one octave higher, like a firefly made of pixels. The sound is
warm, airy and bright, clear in the mid range, never harsh.

The piece starts at full level and keeps it: no intro, no ending, no
fade-out. The last bar leads straight back into the first so the 90-second
