Proud, melancholic instrumental piece for a pixel city - walking down a
busy street, unbothered and a little heartbroken, head held high. 86 BPM
in 4/4 with a steady, unhurried drum groove on every beat from the first
bar to the last.

A full string section plays one sweeping four-bar loop in F major, and the
same loop repeats for the whole piece: a bold, singing line that rises and
falls over the chords F major, C major, D minor, B flat major. Underneath,
a round electric bass follows the roots, a dry kick and a crisp snare on
two and four keep a laid-back hip hop pulse, and a clean electric guitar
holds one long, slowly swelling note.

On every second pass a soft 8-bit square-wave voice doubles the string
melody one octave higher, like the same street seen in pixels. The mix is
wide and warm, clear in the mid range, never harsh.

The piece starts at full level and keeps it: no intro, no ending, no
fade-out. The last bar leads straight back into the first so the 90-second
loop is seamless.

No vocals, no choir, no spoken word. No drops, no risers, no dramatic
climax.
