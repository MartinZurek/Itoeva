Warm instrumental character theme for a small, friendly, open-hearted pixel
creature - the brightest and most welcoming of six personal themes.

Tempo around 92 BPM. Gentle, unhurried groove with a soft brushed snare, a
light rounded kick and quiet shakers - present enough to walk to, never
driving. Acoustic upright piano in the foreground, close-miked and slightly
soft-hammered, playing open major voicings with added 9ths and suspended
seconds; the sustain pedal held long enough that the chords bloom into each
other. Warm upright bass in easy stepwise motion underneath. A clean nylon
guitar doubles the piano an octave up in a few phrases and then steps back.

The melody is the point of this piece: a simple, singable eight-bar tune in a
bright major key, mostly stepwise with one open leap upward, phrased in short
arcs that leave room to breathe between them. It is stated once plainly, then
once with the guitar joining, then plainly again - close enough to a folk song
that a person could learn it by ear and sing along, but played instrumentally.

Light room tone and a soft tape warmth. No vinyl crackle - this piece should
sound a shade cleaner and more present than the town's background music.

Friendly, calm, curious, unguarded. Even dynamics from start to end so the loop
point is inaudible. Plenty of space in the mid range so small sounds and
animations stay audible on top.

No vocals, no rap, no spoken word, no choir, no vocal samples. No drops, no
builds, no risers, no EDM synths, no orchestral or cinematic scoring, no
aggressive transients, no dissonant or unsettled harmony. Nothing that demands
attention.
