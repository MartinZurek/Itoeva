Sunny, carefree instrumental surf and island piece for a pixel beach on a
bright summer afternoon - warm sand, gentle waves, a light breeze and
nowhere to be. 112 BPM in 4/4 with a relaxed, clearly audible pulse on
every beat from the first bar to the last.

A clean electric guitar with a light, round tone plays one simple,
catchy four-bar melody in F major, and the same melody returns for the
whole piece. A strummed ukulele plays the chords F major, D minor,
B-flat major, C major in a repeating cycle, a marimba answers the
melody on every second phrase in the same middle register, a round
electric bass walks gently under it, and soft bongos with a shaker keep
an easy, swaying groove.

In the second half, a soft, rounded triangle-wave 8-bit voice answers
the melody with its own short phrase in the same middle register, like
sunlight drawn in pixels, taking turns with the lead rather than playing
along with it. Halfway through, a contrasting four-bar B phrase at the
same energy gives the main melody a rest - a different lead instrument
and a new chord colour from the same key - and then the main melody
returns. The sound is bright, airy and warm, clear in the mid range,
never harsh.

Harmony and tone: one key from the first bar to the last, simple diatonic
chords in consonant voicings, every voice perfectly in tune with every
other. The lead and the keys stay in the warm middle register, and the few
high notes are soft and rounded. A clean, open tone with generous headroom
and a gentle, soft top end.

The piece starts at full level and keeps it: no intro, no ending, no
fade-out. The last bar leads straight back into the first so the 90-second
loop is seamless.

No vocals, no choir, no spoken word, no wave or seagull sounds. No drops,
no risers, no dramatic climax.
