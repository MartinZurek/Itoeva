Noble, bittersweet instrumental theme for a pixel world at sunrise - quiet
pride, deep calm and a little sorrow for something that is passing, with
hope rising underneath. 70 BPM in 4/4 with a steady, clearly audible pulse
from the first bar to the last.

A warm cello section plays one long, singable eight-bar melody in D minor,
and the same melody returns for the whole piece. A breathy bamboo flute
answers each phrase with a short, simple echo. Soft sustained strings hold
the chords underneath: D minor, B flat major, G minor, A major. Deep taiko
drums mark the first beat of every bar with a softer hit on beat three,
and a plucked koto keeps a gentle eighth-note movement.

On every second pass a soft 8-bit square-wave voice doubles the melody one
octave higher, quiet and warm, like the same memory seen in pixels. The
mix is full and warm, clear in the mid range, never harsh.

The piece starts at full level and keeps it: no intro, no ending, no
fade-out. The last bar leads straight back into the first so the 90-second
loop is seamless.

No vocals, no choir, no spoken word. No drops, no risers, no battle
percussion, no dramatic climax.
