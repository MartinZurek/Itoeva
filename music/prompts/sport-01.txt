Warm instrumental Japanese-influenced jazzy lo-fi hip hop for real movement and
exertion at a pixel sports ground - the same musical family as the town's
daily background, pushed into a brisker, more driven groove without losing
its warmth.

Tempo around 100 BPM. Confident boom bap groove with a punchy but still
soft-edged kick, a crisp brushed snare pushed slightly ahead of the beat, and
busier swung hi-hats that give real forward momentum. Warm Rhodes electric
piano stabs using major 7th and 9th voicings, chopped into short rhythmic
phrases rather than held chords, filtered through a gentle low-pass sweep for
a soulful, sample-worked feel. Melodic upright-style bass that walks with more
motion than the town track and the occasional syncopated push. A breathy alto
saxophone or muted trumpet answers the piano in short two-bar phrases. Light
vinyl crackle and tape saturation for warmth, but every instrument stays
perfectly in tune and in time - no pitch wobble, no wavering intonation, no
warped-tape or dragged-vinyl effect.

Energised, encouraging, purposeful - driven but never anxious or aggressive.
Plenty of space in the mid range so small sounds and animations stay audible
on top. Even dynamics from start to end so the loop point is inaudible.

No vocals, no rap, no spoken word. No drops, no builds, no risers, no EDM
synths, no distorted rock guitars, no orchestral or cinematic scoring, no
aggressive transients or clipping. Warm and driven, never hectic or hostile.
