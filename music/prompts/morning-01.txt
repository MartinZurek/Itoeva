Soft instrumental Japanese-influenced jazzy lo-fi hip hop for early
morning in a quiet pixel town - the same warm family as the daily
background, but airier, slower and just waking up.

Tempo around 72 BPM. Gentle boom bap groove with a soft rounded kick
sitting well back in the mix, a very lightly brushed snare, and airy
shuffling hi-hats with plenty of space between hits. Warm Rhodes electric
piano using major 9th and add9 voicings, played sparsely with long sustain
and lots of room to breathe. Soft rounded upright-style bass entering only
on select downbeats rather than every measure. A soft marimba in its
middle register carries a simple pentatonic melody, answered by a muted
flute in the second half. Light morning room tone, a faint tape hiss,
subtle vinyl crackle, otherwise a clean, open sound.

Calm, fresh, quietly hopeful, unhurried - like a slow exhale before the
day begins. Plenty of space in the mid range so small sounds and
animations stay audible on top. Even dynamics from start to end so the
loop point is inaudible.

Harmony and tone: one key from the first bar to the last, simple diatonic
chords in consonant voicings, every voice perfectly in tune with every
other. The lead and the keys stay in the warm middle register, and the few
high notes are soft and rounded. A clean, open tone with generous headroom
and a gentle, soft top end.

No vocals, no rap, no spoken word. No drops, no builds, no risers, no EDM
synths, no orchestral or cinematic scoring, no aggressive transients, no
busy percussion. Nothing that demands attention or feels rushed.
