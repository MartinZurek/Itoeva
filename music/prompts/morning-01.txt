Soft instrumental Japanese-influenced jazzy lo-fi hip hop for early morning
in a quiet pixel town - the same warm family as the daily background, but
airier, slower and just waking up.

Tempo around 72 BPM. Gentle boom bap groove with a soft rounded kick sitting
well back in the mix, a very lightly brushed snare, and airy shuffling
hi-hats with plenty of space between hits. Warm Rhodes electric piano using
major 9th and add9 voicings, played sparsely with long sustain and lots of
room to breathe. Soft rounded upright-style bass entering only on select
downbeats rather than every measure. A breathy kalimba or soft mallet
instrument carries a simple pentatonic melody, doubled faintly by a muted
flute in the second half. Light morning room tone, gentle tape hiss, subtle
vinyl crackle.

Calm, fresh, quietly hopeful, unhurried - like a slow exhale before the day
begins. Plenty of space in the mid range so small sounds and animations stay
audible on top. Even dynamics from start to end so the loop point is
inaudible.

No vocals, no rap, no spoken word. No drops, no builds, no risers, no EDM
synths, no orchestral or cinematic scoring, no aggressive transients, no
busy percussion. Nothing that demands attention or feels rushed.
