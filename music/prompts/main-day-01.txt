Warm instrumental Japanese-influenced jazzy lo-fi hip hop for a calm pixel town.

Tempo around 82 BPM. Dusty boom bap groove with a soft rounded kick, a dry
restrained snare slightly behind the beat, and organic shuffling hi-hats.
Warm Rhodes electric piano chords using major 7th, minor 7th, 9th and the
occasional 11th voicing. Melodic round upright-style bass that walks gently
between the chords. Sparse vibraphone and clean electric guitar motifs that
answer each other rather than compete. Subtle pentatonic melodic colouring.
Light vinyl crackle and tape saturation, gentle wow and flutter.

Nostalgic, thoughtful, optimistic, unhurried, continuously flowing. Plenty of
space in the mid range so small sounds and animations stay audible on top.

No vocals, no rap, no spoken word. No drops, no builds, no risers, no EDM
synths, no orchestral or cinematic scoring, no aggressive transients. Nothing
that demands attention.
