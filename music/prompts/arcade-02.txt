Warm, glowing instrumental synthwave for a pixel arcade after dark - neon
reflections, a last round before closing time, a little nostalgia. 100 BPM
in 4/4 with a steady, clearly audible drum-machine pulse on every beat
from the first bar to the last.

A bright analog synth lead plays one simple, singable eight-bar melody in
A minor, and the same melody returns for the whole piece. Warm pad chords
hold A minor, F major, C major, G major, a pulsing synth bass plays steady
eighth notes on the roots, and gated drum-machine drums keep time: kick on
every beat, big snare on two and four, closed hi-hats.

On every second pass a soft 8-bit square-wave voice doubles the melody
one octave higher, like a high score blinking in pixels. The sound is
warm, wide and clear in the mid range, never harsh.

The piece starts at full level and keeps it: no intro, no ending, no
fade-out. The last bar leads straight back into the first so the 90-second
loop is seamless.

No vocals, no spoken word, no sound effects. No drops, no risers, no
dramatic climax.
