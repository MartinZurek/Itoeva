Lazy, sunny instrumental piece for fishing at a quiet pixel lake - patience,
warm afternoon light and a happy little hope for a bite. 76 BPM in 4/4
with a relaxed, clearly audible pulse on every beat from the first bar to
the last.

A warm lap slide guitar plays one slow, singable eight-bar melody in G
major, and the same melody returns for the whole piece. A ukulele strums
the chords G major, E minor, C major, D major gently on every beat, a
round upright bass plays one note on beats one and three, and a soft
brushed snare with a quiet shaker keeps a lazy swing.

On every second pass a soft 8-bit square-wave voice doubles the melody
one octave higher, like sunlight glittering on the water in pixels. The
sound is warm, soft and sunny, clear in the mid range, never harsh.

The piece starts at full level and keeps it: no intro, no ending, no
fade-out. The last bar leads straight back into the first so the 90-second
loop is seamless.

No vocals, no whistling, no spoken word, no water sounds. No drops, no
risers, no dramatic climax.
