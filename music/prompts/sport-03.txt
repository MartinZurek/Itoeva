Hard, confident instrumental rap rock beat for a pixel sports ground -
swagger, focus and pure determination, nothing can stop you today. 92 BPM
in 4/4 with a loud, dry, punchy drum break on every beat from the first
bar to the last.

A crunchy overdriven electric guitar plays one short, catchy two-bar riff
in E minor, and the same riff returns for the whole piece, answered by
heavy power-chord stabs on the off-beats. A thick bass locks onto the
kick drum; the drums are big and tight: booming kick, cracking snare on
two and four, busy hi-hats and a short cowbell accent at the end of every
fourth bar.

On every second pass a soft 8-bit square-wave voice doubles the guitar
riff one octave higher, like the same move replayed in pixels. The sound
is loud, tight and punchy, clear in the mid range, never muddy.

The piece starts at full level and keeps it: no intro, no ending, no
fade-out. The last bar leads straight back into the first so the 90-second
loop is seamless.

No vocals, no rap, no spoken word, no scratching. No drops, no risers, no
guitar solo.
