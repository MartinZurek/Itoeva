Restless instrumental character theme for a fast-moving, impatient, hands-on
pixel creature - the rawest and most jagged of six personal themes.

Tempo around 128 BPM, driving straight-eighth pulse. Overdriven, slightly
fuzzy electric guitar carries the main riff with palm-muted low chugs and a
few open power-chord hits. A prominent, gritty electric bass doubles the riff
an octave down and pushes hard against the kick. Drums with a real edge: a
tight cracking snare, a fast hi-hat, and a couple of tom fills that spill
straight back into the beat without slowing it down.

The melody is the point of this piece: a simple, singable eight-bar tune
built mostly from short repeated riff cells with one driving upward push near
the end, phrased so it could be shouted or sung along to a crowd. State it
once on guitar alone, then again with the full band pushing under it, then
once more stripped back before the loop turns over.

Raw, close-miked room tone with light amp hiss - no polish, no vinyl crackle,
this should sound rougher and more present than the town's background music.
Restless, impatient, eager to move. Even energy from start to end so the loop
point is inaudible, but never soft or laid-back.

No vocals, no rap, no spoken word, no choir, no vocal samples. No orchestral
or cinematic scoring, no EDM drops, builds or risers, no clean jazzy chords,
no mellow keys, no brushed or soft-hammered instruments, no sparse or ambient
passages. Nothing calm.
