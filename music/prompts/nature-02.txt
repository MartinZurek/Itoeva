Bright, open instrumental folk piece for a pixel meadow on a windy summer
day - freedom, fresh air and a light heart, walking through tall grass
toward the hills. 104 BPM in 6/8 with a light, bouncing pulse on every
beat from the first bar to the last.

A tin whistle plays one simple, catchy eight-bar melody in D major, and
the same melody returns for the whole piece. A fingerpicked acoustic
guitar plays the chords D major, G major, B minor, A major in a repeating
cycle, a fiddle doubles the melody on every second phrase, and a soft
frame drum with a round bass keeps the rolling rhythm.

On every second pass a soft 8-bit square-wave voice doubles the whistle
melody one octave higher, like the wind drawn in pixels. The sound is
light, airy and warm, clear in the mid range, never harsh.

The piece starts at full level and keeps it: no intro, no ending, no
fade-out. The last bar leads straight back into the first so the 90-second
loop is seamless.

No vocals, no choir, no spoken word, no bird sounds. No drops, no risers,
no dramatic climax.
